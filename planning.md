# Sun of Prosperity — Open House

## Theme

Sun of Prosperity — an interactive Open House concept combining Tarot-inspired houses, visitor flow and visual identity.

## Houses

1. Sun
2. Star
3. Empress
4. Wheel
5. Moon
6. Strength
7. World
8. Magician
9. Lovers
10. Hierophant

## Operations

- 20 staff, 2 people per house
- Floors 12 and 14
- Elevator flow
- Rain scenario
- No-show scenario
- Gold / orange / yellow visual direction
- Flower / butterfly / Tarot visual motifs

## Visitor flow

```text
Arrival -> Orientation -> House Selection -> Activity -> Exit / Next House
```

## Planning value

The project demonstrates requirement gathering, resource allocation, visitor-flow design and scenario planning.
